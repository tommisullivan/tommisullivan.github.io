Privacy Policy

Music Studio · last updated 19 October 2026

Music Studio is a personal application that its owner runs on their own computer to manage their own YouTube channels. Nobody else uses it. This policy explains what data it handles.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Music Studio receives access to the owner's YouTube channels through Google's OAuth sign-in. With that access it:

  • uploads the owner's videos and thumbnails;
  • sets their titles, descriptions, playlists and publishing times;
  • reads the channel's name and ID;
  • reads the channels' YouTube Analytics reports, such as views, watch time and traffic sources.

How it's used

Only to publish and manage the owner's own videos and to show the owner how their videos are doing. It's not used for advertising, profiling, or anything else.

Where it's stored

Sign-in tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Music Studio has no servers, keeps no copies elsewhere, and this website sets no cookies and does no tracking.

Sharing

Data is never sold, shared with or sent to anyone. The only exception is YouTube itself: when the owner publishes a video, it goes to YouTube.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which ends Music Studio's access straight away.

Google and YouTube

Music Studio uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Music Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
