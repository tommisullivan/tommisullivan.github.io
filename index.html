Music Studio

A personal tool for publishing original music to its owner's own YouTube channels.

Music Studio is a private application that its owner runs on their own computer. It helps them upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channels, and shows them those channels' statistics. It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs.

It isn't a public service. There are no sign-ups and no user accounts, and nobody other than the owner can use it.

Read the privacy policy. Music Studio uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply.
